L&D DESIGNS — PRICING GUIDE
============================
(Internal reference — not for sharing directly)

STARTER SITE — £150 one-off
- 1-3 pages (Home, About, Contact)
- Mobile responsive
- Basic Google presence
- Contact form
- Social links
- Turnaround: 3-5 days
- Best for: Barbers, cleaners, dog groomers, small trades

STANDARD SITE — £250 one-off
- Up to 5 pages
- Gallery / portfolio section
- Google Maps embed
- Basic SEO setup
- Turnaround: 5-7 days
- Best for: Builders, mechanics, restaurants, beauty salons

PRO SITE — £400 one-off
- Up to 8 pages
- Online booking or contact form
- Full SEO setup
- Google My Business setup
- Turnaround: 7-14 days
- Best for: Trades wanting to rank on Google

ADD-ONS:
- Logo design: £50
- Google My Business setup: £50
- Monthly maintenance: £20/month
- Basic Google Ads setup: £75 (plus ad spend)

NEGOTIATION GUIDE:
- Don't go below £100 for any site
- If they push back on price, offer payment in 2 instalments
- First message always says "from £150" — doesn't commit you
- If they're a referral, you can do a small discount
