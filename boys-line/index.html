About Us

More than just a haircut

Boys Line is Standish's premier barbershop — a place where every client gets the attention they deserve. Whether you're after a sharp fade, a classic cut, or a full grooming experience, our skilled barbers bring precision and passion to every chair.

Located on Preston Road in the heart of Standish, Wigan — we've built a reputation for exceptional quality and a welcoming atmosphere that keeps our clients coming back.

  • Expert barbers with years of experience
  • Cuts for all ages — from kids to gents
  • Full beard grooming & hot towel shaves
  • Walk-ins welcome · Appointments available via WhatsApp
