How It Works — Step by Step
1
Find your leads — Open the Universal Lead Finder, pick a business type (e.g. restaurants), run all 3 cells. A spreadsheet downloads automatically.
→ Open Lead Finder
2
Build your outreach dashboard — Open the WhatsApp tool, upload the spreadsheet from Step 1, run cells 2 & 3. An HTML file downloads.
→ Open Outreach Tool
3
Start messaging — Open outreach_dashboard.html in your browser. Click the green WhatsApp button on each business — message is pre-typed, just hit Send. Cards track your progress automatically.
4
Close the deal — When someone replies, mark them as Interested in the dashboard. Add notes on price quoted, follow-up date, etc. Check back daily.

Tips to Stay Safe & Get Replies
  • Use WhatsApp Business (free app) — designed for outreach, less likely to get flagged than a personal number
  • Send a maximum of 50 messages per day — don't blast everyone at once
  • The message is short and personal on purpose — long corporate messages get ignored or reported
  • If someone says not interested, mark them Not interested in the dashboard so you never message them again
  • Follow up anyone who doesn't reply after 3–5 days — most clients need a nudge
  • Use the Boys Line site as your portfolio proof — "here's one I built recently" closes deals
