Common Questions

Got a question?

Do I need to provide content?

Not much. Just send over any photos you have (even phone photos are fine) and a few bullet points about your business — services, opening hours, that sort of thing. Dylan will write the copy and design the layout for you.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit (£75) is taken upfront to start the work, with the remaining £75 due before the site goes live. After that it's £15/month which covers hosting and keeping the site updated. Payment by bank transfer or PayPal.

Can I update the site myself later?

Yes. Depending on how it’s built, Dylan can set up a simple content system so you can change text and photos yourself. Or just drop him a message — small updates are usually free or very low cost.

What if I’m not happy with the design?

Two rounds of revisions are included as standard. If after both rounds you’re still not satisfied, you get a full refund — no questions asked. Dylan’s goal is a site you’re genuinely proud of. Chat to him first if you’re unsure.
